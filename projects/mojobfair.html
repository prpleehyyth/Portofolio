Mojobfair — Job Fair Recruitment System

Year
2025
Role
IT Intern / Fullstack Developer
Stack
Laravel 12, PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS

Overview

A comprehensive web-based job recruitment platform developed for Dinas Komunikasi dan Informatika (Diskominfo) Kota Mojokerto to support the regional Job Fair program and facilitate online job searching for the public.

The challenge

  • Diskominfo needed a central digital platform to manage job vacancies, employer accounts, and applicant submissions during Job Fair events.
  • High traffic volume during job fair events required performance testing and scalable database management.

The approach

  • Developed authentication, job vacancy management, and applicant processing modules using Laravel 12.
  • Designed clean, responsive user interfaces using Tailwind CSS.
  • Managed relational data and application schemas with PostgreSQL.
  • Ran load and stress tests on OPD web services to ensure stability during peak traffic.

The result

  • Streamlined the application process for job seekers in Mojokerto.
  • Provided Diskominfo and participating companies with a unified dashboard for applicant management.
